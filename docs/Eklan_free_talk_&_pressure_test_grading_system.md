    C. Scenario-based Grading
    1. ICU Emergency / Critical SItuation Scenario (Handling emergency/critical situation)

#
Observable Behaviour
What AI Is Looking To Evaluate
1
Recognizes patient deterioration quickly
Identifies emergency signs such as low oxygen saturation, chest pain, respiratory distress, hypotension, or confusion without delay
2
Provides immediate appropriate intervention
Initiates correct first actions such as increasing oxygen, monitoring vital signs, positioning patient safely, or assessing symptoms
3
Uses calm and reassuring communication
Speaks calmly, reduces patient anxiety, and maintains emotional control during the emergency
4
Gives clear patient instructions
Uses short, direct instructions such as “Take slow deep breaths” or “Please stay still”
5
Escalates appropriately and promptly
Calls the doctor, respiratory therapist, rapid response team, or additional support without unnecessary delay
6
Uses professional ICU terminology
Correctly uses terms like oxygen saturation, blood pressure, respiratory distress, chest tightness, or heart rhythm
7
Maintains organized and safe communication
Communicates clearly under pressure, prioritizes patient safety, and avoids panic or disorganized responses

    2. Admission/Introduction Scenario (Introducing oneself and admitting a patient)

#
Observable Behaviour
What AI Is Looking To Evaluate
1
Introduces self clearly
States name and role confidently and professionally (e.g., “Hello, I’m Nurse Yoojeong, and I’ll be taking care of you today.”)
2
Explains role and purpose of interaction
Clearly explains why they are there and what will happen during admission
3
Confirms patient identity correctly
Uses at least two identifiers appropriately (e.g., name and date of birth) to support patient safety
4
Explains the admission process clearly
Gives simple, organized explanation of procedures, monitoring, paperwork, or next steps
5
Uses polite and professional tone
Maintains respectful, calm, and welcoming bedside communication
6
Encourages patient questions or concerns
Invites the patient to ask questions and demonstrates openness to communication
7
Establishes rapport and patient comfort
Helps the patient feel welcomed, safe, and supported during admission

    3. Small Talk with a Patient

#
Observable Behaviour
What AI Is Looking To Evaluate
1
Maintains friendly and approachable tone
Speaks warmly, calmly, and in a way that helps the patient feel comfortable
2
Uses appropriate social language
Uses natural conversational phrases suitable for healthcare settings without sounding overly formal or robotic
3
Demonstrates active listening
Responds appropriately to what the patient says and shows attentiveness during conversation
4
Responds naturally in conversation
Avoids scripted or awkward responses and maintains smooth conversational flow
5
Builds rapport with the patient
Creates positive connection through empathy, humor, encouragement, or shared conversation appropriately
6
Maintains professionalism throughout interaction
Keeps appropriate boundaries, respectful tone, and professional bedside behavior
7
Encourages patient comfort and engagement
Helps the patient feel relaxed, included, and willing to continue communication

    4. Nurse-to-Nurse Handover

#
Observable Behaviour
What AI Is Looking To Evaluate
1
Gives concise and focused report
Communicates important information clearly without unnecessary details or excessive rambling
2
Organizes information logically
Presents information in clear sequence (e.g., diagnosis → events → treatment → monitoring needs)
3
Includes critical patient details
Mentions important clinical information such as diagnosis, vital changes, medications, procedures, safety concerns, or pending tasks
4
Uses SBAR/ISBAR communication structure appropriately
Demonstrates structured handoff communication with clear situation, background, assessment, and recommendations
5
Answers clarification questions accurately
Responds appropriately and confidently when the receiving nurse asks follow-up questions
6
Confirms understanding and continuity of care
Ensures the receiving nurse understands key concerns, priorities, and ongoing monitoring needs
7
Maintains professional and collaborative communication
Uses respectful tone, teamwork language, and professional handoff behavior throughout interaction

    5. Declining a Request Professionally

#
Observable Behavior
What AI Is Looking To Evaluate
1
Maintains respectful and calm tone
Speaks politely and professionally without sounding rude, dismissive, or irritated
2
States limitation or refusal clearly
Clearly explains why the request cannot be fulfilled without being vague or overly apologetic
3
Avoids confrontation or defensive language
Maintains composure and avoids arguing, blaming, or escalating tension
4
Provides alternative solution or assistance
Offers another option, compromise, or next step when appropriate
5
Maintains professionalism throughout interaction
Uses appropriate workplace communication and respectful boundaries
6
Demonstrates teamwork and collaboration
Shows willingness to support colleagues or patients even when declining the request
7
Communicates confidently and appropriately
Delivers refusal clearly and professionally without hesitation, confusion, or passive-aggressive tone

    6. Phone Communication with Doctor

#
Observable Behavior
What AI Is Looking To Evaluate
1
Identifies self, unit, and patient appropriately
Clearly introduces themselves, unit/department, and patient information at the start of the call
2
States reason for call immediately
Quickly explains why they are calling without unnecessary delays or excessive background information
3
Uses concise SBAR/ISBAR communication
Organizes information logically using Situation, Background, Assessment, and Recommendation
4
Gives accurate and relevant patient data
Provides correct vital signs, symptoms, assessment findings, medications, or changes in condition
5
Requests recommendation, orders, or action appropriately
Clearly states what is needed from the physician (evaluation, medication order, intervention, etc.)
6
Confirms and repeats orders correctly
Uses read-back/closed-loop communication to verify physician instructions accurately
7
Maintains calm, professional, and organized communication
Speaks confidently, remains composed under pressure, and communicates efficiently
